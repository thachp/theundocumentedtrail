# Create immigration journey

Preserve one coherent product across journeys; vary the history and household, not the basic interaction contract. This skill grants no authority to contact people, commission paid work, merge, deploy or publish.

1. Read checkout `AGENTS.md`, current branch/status, issue brief and claims, and open dependent PRs. Skip another worker's claimed work; claim only the authorized ticket. Use a separate branch; preserve real saves. Establish brief-only versus implementation scope. For an out-of-checkout brief, record the inspected head and missing ticket/dependency access; do not create a branch or claim an invented issue. Recheck file names against the checkout.
2. Read [the consistency contract](references/consistency-contract.md) before drafting; consult [first-five evidence](references/first-five-review.md) for strengths and pitfalls, not historical authority.
3. Copy [the authoring template](assets/journey-brief.md). Pin departure/transit/arrival periods and legal jurisdictions. Read the underlying sources, recording publication/effective dates, exact sections and limitations against individual claims. Prefer contemporary primary evidence and distinguish later interpretation. A search snippet, inaccessible URL or another story is not verification. Remove, qualify or hold unsupported claims. Distinguish sourced conditions from every fictional person, conversation, resource quantity, delay and outcome.
4. Start every new journey with exactly four named traveling-party members: a family or another coherent group, each with distinct identity, relationships, aims and a meaningful story role. Do not force a nuclear family or fill a slot with a stereotype. This fixes starting size, not later composition: preserve justified separation, births, reunification or loss and record continuity honestly. Track ages and elapsed years, relationships and visual continuity across every scene; attribute translated dialogue as paraphrase until checked. Mark unknown transit connections and hold procedural claims rather than inventing precise stops. Build household continuity and meaningful disagreement: skills, personal aims, care, ordinary life, relatives staying, transit communities and receiving communities. Attribute speech accurately; represent outside perspectives through situated evidence, not false balance or ethnic/crime generalizations. Do not lift distinctive testimony or imply endorsement. Separate survival, arrival, registration, permission, asylum, residence and belonging. Never make moral choices earn legal approval.
5. Choose the existing implementation path using [the repository map](references/repository-map.md). Prefer modular authored story data for bounded historical households; use survival mechanics only when the brief needs them. Do not create a separate historical-story UI or a new engine. Record schema limitations before expanding scope.
6. Plan cover plus changing scene-specific heroes, continuity sheet, alt/crop and provenance before implementation. Inspect generated art; preserve people, period clothing/transport/objects and non-graphic tone. Record generator/source, prompts/references, rights/limitations, hashes and dimensions. If image tools are missing, report early and prepare prompts; do not substitute unrelated art or call placeholder work complete.
7. Implement through normal chooser and shared Guided/Planning controllers, geographical event coordinates, sourced optional context and isolated saves. Preserve existing saves, archives, direct links and pending dependent work. No score/rank. Keep consequences after commitment and navigation reversible where supported.
8. Run [the verification matrix](references/consistency-contract.md#verification-matrix), appropriate existing tests and build. Use fresh isolated browser storage, never clear real saves. Record exact head, viewport/mode/routes, actual evidence and failures. Test accessibility honestly: keyboard, zoom and reduced-motion checks are not screen-reader testing. Record unrun checks as unrun; use any suitable supported browser/AT combination, not a mandatory OS.
9. Deliver source/fiction ledger, art provenance, test evidence, known gaps and a draft PR when authorized. Recommend community, language, historical or legal review where useful; require it only if the brief explicitly does. No invented certification, paid-review gate or automatic merge/publication. A blocked check remains visible; an owner waiver must identify its scope rather than fabricate a pass.

For brief-only work, stop after a reviewable completed template and validation plan; do not invent implemented assets or test results.
