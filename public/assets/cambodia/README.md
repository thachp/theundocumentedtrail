# Cambodia fictional illustrations — four-person edition

Original AI-generated illustrations created for this prototype on 18 October 2026. They depict the invented Khmer Cambodian relatives Dara, Vann, Maly and Sophea; they are not archival photographs, survivor portraits, verified camp layouts or community-endorsed representations. `cover.webp` is the late-1979 household reference. Every scene C01–C09 now has a distinct illustration; C02 is a non-graphic symbolic recollection, not a reconstruction of atrocities.

The original three-person cover was inspected and used to add the fourth invented person; the resulting four-person cover guided every scene. Period clothing, faces and ordinary activities are interpretive rather than externally certified. Source images stay in the generation workspace; `provenance.json` records their names/hashes, exact prompts, references, output dimensions/hashes, quality, inspection and limitations. WebP derivatives retain native dimensions (1672×941; C07 is1671×941), each below200kB, total1,809,238bytes. Cover-based portrait crops are declared in `src/cambodiaJourney.js`; runtime alt text identifies each scene and its fictional status.

Unmodified three-person assets remain under `public/assets/legacy-households/cambodia/` for original runs. No third-party archival imagery or oral-history media is reproduced. Fine cultural/age accuracy and external review remain unclaimed.
