## Capacity exploration and consequences

The model is expressly invented: four requested appointment-work units, three staff units and three room units, funded from the fictional voluntary service’s operating funds. These are workload units, not four people to rank, empirical local data, currency or the NAO spending amount. Interpretation/private explanation can require more than one unit. No reader decides whose personhood or case is worthy; any urgency assessment is the fictional staff’s private responsibility.

Three alternatives expose assumptions and trade-offs without a winning plan:

- Keep the current funded session: three available units, one unmet today and later under unchanged assumptions. Predictable hours help some users/staff; waiting creates burdens for others.
- Explore an extension: four units today **only if** staff consent, funds and room access are available. Those hours/funds cannot also cover other work; it is not unpaid goodwill by default.
- Explore another future session: today’s constraint remains; later capacity can increase **only if** funded staff and a room are secured. A funding application is not an award. Holding requests constant is an exploration assumption, not a prediction.

Capacity is the minimum of staff and room time; unmet work cannot go below zero. Immediate and later results are separate. Continuing operation still needs resources; possible work/volunteering contributions depend on circumstances and permissions and cannot be promised to cover a particular service’s costs. No legal route, work right, future employment or official decision is created.

The buttons explore local UI alternatives only. No cash, food, trust, health, social climate, time, flags, score, save version, journal entry, route, map marker or outcome changes. There is no storage write or call to a gameplay reducer. Main decision descriptions and concealed numerical consequences remain untouched. The collapsed disclosure can be ignored or closed; reopening/exploring produces no resource reward. Existing saves need no migration.
