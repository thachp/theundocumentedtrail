# Make Cambodia a branching journey of supplies, travel and care

Draft PR target: `thachp/theundocumentedtrail`, base `main`, head `fix/cambodia-event-journey`.

Cambodia's expanded journey previously advanced mainly through narrated scenes, with zero-day choice metadata and blanket health changes. New runs now branch through travel delays, follow-up care and supply shortages; travel consumes daily food and water, active/travel days accumulate, and effort changes individual relatives' health and conditions. Household endings reflect the resulting care needs and means. The established survival/admission premises remain independent of player performance.

Rules 1/2, original editions, archived runs, irreversible committed choices and explicit scoped restart are preserved. The chooser no longer displays completed-run lists, and repeated source/fiction labels are consolidated. Fix the actual Aurelia card reference by keeping the detail card within the viewport after scrolling, bounding its portrait and supporting keyboard dismissal/focus return.

Validation: full `npm test` (193 passing), production/Sites build, desktop/mobile browser matrix, genuine 200% Chromium zoom, repeated confirmation cancellation and refresh/history flows, explicit restart, old-save replay and archive protection. Evidence and exact limitations: `docs/cambodia-event-revision.md` and `artifacts/cambodia-events/`.

Screen-reader testing was not performed. No merge or release is requested by this draft; deployment requires separate release approval.
