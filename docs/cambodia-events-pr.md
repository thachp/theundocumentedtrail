# Make Cambodia an event-driven journey and restore its compact layout

Draft PR target: `thachp/theundocumentedtrail`, base `main`, head `fix/cambodia-event-journey`.

Cambodia's expanded journey previously advanced mainly through narrated scenes, with zero-day choice metadata and blanket health changes. New runs now branch through travel delays, follow-up care and supply shortages; travel consumes daily food and water, active/travel days accumulate, and effort changes individual relatives' health and conditions. Household endings reflect the resulting care needs and means. The established survival/admission premises remain independent of player performance.

Rules 1/2, original editions, archived runs, irreversible committed choices and explicit scoped restart are preserved. The chooser no longer displays completed-run lists, and repeated source/fiction labels are consolidated. Restore expanded Planning’s parchment map on the left and scene/decisions on the right, shorten Guided’s extra-tall stage to the shared minimum, and compact the phone hero/map with supplementary geography in the existing disclosure. Preserve natural scrolling at small widths and 200% zoom. Fix the actual Aurelia card reference by keeping the detail card within the viewport after scrolling, bounding its portrait and supporting keyboard dismissal/focus return.
