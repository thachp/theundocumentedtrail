## Chronology and route

The household leaves western Cambodia in late November 1979, reaches Khao-I-Dang in December after its 21 November opening, waits during 1980–81, receives fictional U.S. admission in 1981 and rebuilds in Long Beach in 1981–82. The ending may continue to a January/March 1985 parallel chapter: letters about an invented friend’s circumstances in Site One and Site Two. The four remain in California. The chapter does not assert that the same friend moved between both sites, and does not offer a Site Two-to-U.S. application route. It explicitly distinguishes those contexts from Khao-I-Dang.

Regional map anchors are deliberately broad; no home, crossing, processing hub, camp entrance or flight is invented. The Southern California land panel derives from the same Natural Earth/world-atlas source as existing maps; other land regions are unchanged. Optional branch locations appear only when visited. Letters about camps leave the current marker in Long Beach.

## Mechanics and persistence

Resources carry between occasions: exchange units (not dollars or baht), food units, water units, stress, each person’s health and cohesion, plus elapsed planning turns. These use the shared game field names; no phone/medicine or generic enforcement exposure is surfaced for this story. Time units are attention/effort abstractions, not historical elapsed days: authored date transitions establish years. Health and cohesion are bounded; no mortality roll or legal gate. Low carried food adds fatigue during later care/work scenes. A repair exchange pays once, costs rest and can unlock a bounded extra-work negotiation. Theft recovery and water collection can open local scenes. Sources and summary alternatives remain available without a timed/reflex task.

Choice descriptions disclose qualitative constraints; exact effects appear only after commitment. Back rebuilds from retained history rather than refunding or stacking rewards. Save validation derives resources/flags from valid historical choices and rejects inconsistent routes/newer versions. Edition3 uses `undocumented-trail:review:cambodia-1979:v3` and `:archive:v3`; v1 and v2 story/state/art are frozen and separately resumable. Direct links preserve edition across mode switches. Existing users resume their old edition until explicitly switching or restarting. Unknown saves and archives are retained, with writes disabled where applicable.
